Core interaction is step 3: the daily amount is an input field with −/+ steppers, so customers can type a precise figure or nudge it. As the budget rises, bigger loans appear — turning "how much can I get?" into "what can I afford?". The dimmed over-budget row is a gentle upsell that never hides options. Everything stays inside one mental model: daily payment.
