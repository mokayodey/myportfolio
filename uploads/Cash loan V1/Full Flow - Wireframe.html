Two phases, one path: screens 1–3 are the affordability-first selection (lead with daily payment, list loans that fit, dimmed over-budget upsell). Screens 4–7 are the start-date sub-flow grafted onto the review step — the inline card keeps it optional and out of the way, and presets handle 90% in one tap while the calendar covers the rest. Numbers shown: a KES 12,000 loan at KES 96/day, start moved to 27 Jun (an 8-day grace).
