Filtering on amount vs. daily payment — 3 flows

Three ways to let customers find a loan. A is today's flat list. B keeps amount as the primary filter (improved from testing). C flips to affordability-first — daily payment leads and the amount falls out as the result, sidestepping the "bigger loan = higher daily" confusion entirely.

How it works today

Flow C
Affordability-first

Flip it: ask the daily budget first, then show which loan amounts fit. Daily payment becomes the primary filter and the amount is the result — the closest match to how customers actually decide. No amount chips to plant the "bigger = pricier" assumption.
