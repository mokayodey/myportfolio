Affordability-first · variations

Six ways to lead with "what can you pay?"

All keep daily payment as the anchor — they differ in how the customer enters it (type, tap, drag, speak), and how matching loans come back (list, plans, single tuned loan).

Variation A
Daily presets

Zero typing. Tap a round daily amount to start. Lowest-effort entry — best for the low-literacy / low-confidence users testing flagged. "Other" opens the keypad.

How to read these: A & F lower the effort/literacy barrier (tap or chat, no keyboard). B fits repayment to income cadence and links to the start-date flow. C & D change the output — named plans vs one tunable loan. E adds a responsible-lending layer. They're combinable: e.g. A's presets → C's three plans, or B's rhythm → D's tuning.
