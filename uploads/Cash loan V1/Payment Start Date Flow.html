Open decisions to flag: (1) Does a grace period add a small fee, or is it free? If it costs, the review screen must show the bump in shillings. (2) Cap the window — I've used 14 days. (3) Default to Tomorrow, not the latest date, so the safe option is the easy one. The whole sub-flow is optional: presets handle 90% in two taps; the calendar is only for the few who want a specific day.
